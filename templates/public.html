Verify an academic certificate

Check in seconds whether a certificate is genuine, issued by a recognised institution and still valid. No account needed, and the check is backed by a tamper-proof blockchain ledger.

Enter the details exactly as printed on the certificate. Even one changed character makes verification fail.

Paste the 64-character fingerprint printed on the certificate. Scanning its QR code opens the result directly.

1 · FingerprintThe certificate details are turned into a unique SHA-256 fingerprint.
2 · Ledger lookupWe check that the fingerprint was recorded and signed by the issuing institution.
3 · IntegrityThe block, its Merkle root and chain links are re-checked, along with revocation.
