How it works

The five ideas behind the project.

1
FingerprintThe certificate details are hashed with SHA-256. Only this hash goes on-chain.
2
SignThe institution signs the hash with its private Ed25519 key.
3
Pending poolThe signed transaction waits until a block is mined.
4
MineTransactions are summarised in a Merkle root and the block is sealed with proof of work.
5
VerifyAnyone recomputes the hash and checks the ledger, issuer, signature and revocation status.

🧪 Hash playground - the avalanche effect

Change a single character and see how completely the SHA-256 fingerprint changes. This is why a forged certificate can never match the ledger.
